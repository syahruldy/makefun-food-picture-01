# Tonkotsu ramen, yolk mid-drip — food-picture-01 (round 3)

The leaderboard's top five are all tonkotsu ramen, so this entry
does not fight the dish — it takes the winning frame and pushes its
two most dynamic details further than any of them. The noodle pull
is a full vertical column rising out of the bowl, and the twin jammy
eggs are caught with the yolk falling in one long stream down onto
the noodles. Around them: a fan of thick charred chashu, a chili-oil
swirl with golden droplets on the creamy broth, a tall nori sheet,
visible steam, and warm bokeh behind a speckled dark bowl.

`main.py` copies the bundled picture (`art.jpg`) to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`. No network, no randomness, the same
bytes on every run.
